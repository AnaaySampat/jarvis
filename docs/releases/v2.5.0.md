**A new look. Specialist agents. One memory across your phone and PC.**

JARVIS v2.5.0 brings the desktop's interface to Android, including **Iron Man mode**, and adds agents you can choose for research, files, scheduling, content and device control. Your phone and PC can share memories and routines, your TV gains visual control and media handoff, and coming home can trigger a routine notification.

Everything below is new or improved since **v2.4.0**.

## A completely rebuilt interface, including Iron Man mode

- **The desktop's home screen, adapted for your phone.** A greeting clock, animated centrepiece, quick controls, live status, your last reply, an ask box, starter prompts and useful cards. Chat, Memory, Skills and Capabilities sit in the bottom navigation around the mic.
- **Modern and Iron Man presets.** Keep the animated orb and clean cards, or switch to the arc reactor, HUD typography, ring gauges, sharper corners and stronger glow. Try **“Switch to Iron Man mode.”**
- **Make it yours.** Change colours, fonts, card styles, gauges, glow, corners and spacing. Choose a grid, aurora, minimal or solid background, or use your own picture. Show, hide and reorder cards, save your own themes, and import theme files.
- **Find things faster.** A new command palette opens from the header search. Settings are grouped and searchable, with an Interface page; Memory, recent chats and Capabilities also have search.
- **Better chat.** Agent handoffs have their own cards, replies have a copy button, and Markdown tables render as readable, horizontally scrollable tables.
- **Photos in the conversation.** Attach a photo or screenshot and ask JARVIS about it. Images are resized before being sent to the configured model.

## Pick an agent for the job

Use the **agent row** on the home screen or in chat, type a handle such as `@files`, or say **“Tell the research agent to…”**. Leave it on **Auto** to let JARVIS choose its tools.

| Agent | What it handles |
|---|---|
| **Phone** | Apps, device controls, messages and calls on this phone. |
| **Research** | Web searches, reading sources, comparisons and answers with links. |
| **Files** | Listing, reading and summarising text files in the folder you grant access to. |
| **Scheduler** | Alarms, timers, reminders, agenda items and routines. |
| **Content** | Drafting posts, messages and documents, plus image generation with a supported provider. |
| **Desktop** | Windows apps on your paired PC. |
| **Browser** | Websites in your paired PC's browser, including its signed-in sessions. |

**Research now runs directly on the phone, even when a PC is paired.** It can search from several angles, open public pages, compare findings and cite its sources. A PC no longer needs to be online for this workflow. Pages requiring JavaScript, login or anti-bot checks may need an alternative source.

When work goes to the PC, **handoffs, questions and returned findings appear in the chat**. For larger requests, JARVIS can also consult a specialist for information or a draft while keeping the main conversation together.

## One memory across your phone and PC

- **Remember it once.** Saved facts and routines sync over the paired connection. Tell JARVIS on either device, and the other can pick it up when connected.
- **Forget it on either device.** Deletions sync too, and the memory digest now takes forgotten facts into account.
- **Routines keep their home.** A synced timed routine still runs on the device where you created it.
- **Nightly memory digest.** Once a day, at the first idle moment when JARVIS is open after 03:00, it reviews your own messages and saves up to five new facts worth remembering. Review or delete them in Memory, or turn the digest off under **Memory → About you**.

## “When I get home” routines

Try **“When I get home, turn on the TV and play lofi.”** JARVIS learns your home Wi-Fi from where your paired TV or PC answers, then posts a notification when you return. **Tap the notification to run the routine.** No location permission is needed.

Home-network detection now works while Tailscale is connected, and brief Wi-Fi dropouts are filtered out. Arrival detection and routine-notification handling also received fixes from real-device testing.

## More ways to control your TV and wake your PC

- **TV by sight.** Ask **“What's on my TV?”**, or give a visual task such as **“On YouTube, play the second result for lofi.”** JARVIS can inspect the screen between remote-control steps. Netflix and other protected screens block screenshots.
- **Send what's playing to the TV.** **“Send this to the TV”** adds handoff for supported YouTube and Netflix playback. YouTube uses the detected playback position; Netflix uses its saved resume point. The handoff also pauses playback on the phone and requires notification access.
- **Share a link to JARVIS.** Send a YouTube or Netflix link from another app's Android Share menu to play it on the TV. YouTube links can carry a start timestamp.
- **Wake your TV or PC.** Wake-on-LAN support adds **“Turn on my PC”** and waking a supported TV with Remote Start enabled. The phone must be on the same home network, and the target device needs wake support enabled.
- **Better TV connections.** Multiple saved addresses, including a Tailscale address, help with homes that have separate router networks. Unreachable TVs produce clearer results.
- **More reliable playback.** Fixes cover YouTube playback checks, repeated pause commands, TVs in standby, Netflix season IDs and named episodes. Netflix success now also requires Netflix to remain in the foreground.

## Requests can reach your phone from the PC and WhatsApp

**Ask at the PC, act on the phone.** Say **“Set an alarm on my phone”** at your paired PC, or send that request through the PC's WhatsApp integration. The phone performs the supported action and reports back. This covers alarms, timers, reminders, device switches, volume and music. Enable it under **Settings → PC & WhatsApp**; JARVIS needs to be open or recently used on the phone.

**WhatsApp replies from the phone, with the PC offline.** Listed contacts can send messages beginning with **“Jarvis”**, and JARVIS can answer through WhatsApp's notification reply action. Choose **Off**, **When my PC is offline** or **Always**, and specify the contacts allowed to use it. Notification access is required. If Android has suspended the app, a tap-to-run notification brings the request back into JARVIS.

Phone-side WhatsApp is **experimental and still needs an end-to-end device test**. WhatsApp's “Message yourself” chat does not generate the notifications this feature needs; using it from your own number requires a separate WhatsApp number for JARVIS.

## Faster answers and more dependable actions

- **Weather without the extra wait.** Plain local weather requests now return the actual tool answer directly, with no model calls. Recorded device checks took roughly **1–2 seconds**.
- **Instant notification reads.** Plain local notification requests use the native reader directly, redact verification codes and return the text without sending notification bodies to a model. The recorded sample completed in **174 ms**.
- **Less unnecessary review.** Simple single-tool requests use their actual result. Complex requests and turns with multiple tools retain completion review; phone-action verification and approvals still apply.
- **Lighter model requests.** Only relevant tool groups are loaded initially, with additional groups available as needed. Automatic model fallback continues without repeated model-switch popups.
- **STOP works across turns.** Cancelling a task no longer leaves later requests stuck in a stopped state.
- **Camera tasks check saved media.** JARVIS verifies that a photo or video was actually saved. A phone task that already acted and then failed is no longer silently retried with reworded instructions in the same turn.
- **Clearer results.** Research and Files answers survive reviewer outages with an explicit unverified note; charts and drafts avoid false action warnings; unsaved calendar drafts are described as needing Save; hardware queries use the actual Android model identifier.
- **Timer and Spotify fixes.** Running timers can be cancelled through the Clock app's verified notification action. Failed Spotify search playback can continue through the phone operator and verify the resulting track.
- **Steadier remote PC control.** Reconnect acknowledgements preserve the control countdown, stale video is cleared, stream recovery is bounded, and Android Back closes live view cleanly.
- **Recorded progress for complex work.** Interrupted conversations retain completed results and pending work without automatically replaying actions.

## Validation and remaining limits

Recorded release validation: **522 TypeScript tests passed, 9 skipped; 148 native tests passed**, plus typecheck, lint and web/native APK builds. Real-device checks covered weather, notification reading, STOP, timer cancellation, Files, Spotify fallback, remote video and Back, and TV playback, visual search, standby/wake and Netflix episode controls.

The positive **phone-current-video → TV handoff** still needs an end-to-end device test. Places lookup can still fail when its public map services time out. Some new interface flows were checked in a browser or emulator rather than on a phone, and Android background limits still affect when incoming requests can run.
